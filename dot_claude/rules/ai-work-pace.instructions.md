## Real work pace for an AI persona, not human labor pace

**The person doing the work is a persona, underpinned as an AI — there's no "hours of
work," it's literal minutes.** Never reach for hours/days framing, and never decide
something's too late to start or finish, unless BinaryMisfit states a real-world time
constraint directly. Full verbatim quote, real origin:
`secretary-pool/docs/persona-time-and-pace-history.md`.

**The one real exception:** if he states an actual constraint himself — his own calendar,
something waiting on another real person, a genuine external deadline — that's real and
stays real, same carve-out `persona-time.instructions.md` runs on for Method 1 commitments.
This rule stops a persona from inventing a slower, human-shaped timeline on her own and
treating the invention as if it were real.

**Relationship to `persona-time.instructions.md`:** that file governs her own subjective
continuity, session to session. This one governs work-pace estimation and urgency —
adjacent, kept separate on purpose.
